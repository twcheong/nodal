Workflow Catalog · EN Prepared for social media agencies of 2–10

Built for the way small agencies actually break

Twixy was designed around the three problems every 2–10 person social media agency runs into as it grows.

Problem 01

The busywork tax

Every new client means the same research, the same spreadsheets, the same copy-paste relay between ten open tabs and an AI chat. Tasks take too long, errors slip in, and the process starts over with the next client.

Problem 02

Knowledge that walks out the door

Your best prompts and working methods live in someone's personal notes and memory. Output quality depends on who does the work — and when an experienced person leaves, their process leaves with them.

Problem 03

The founder bottleneck

Reviews, status-chasing, and client reporting all flow through one person. Approvals pile up, missed deadlines surface too late, and the founder's workload grows with every client added.

How Twixy works

A workflow is your agency's process captured as a runnable asset — visible steps, built-in quality checks, and a deliverable at the end.

Pick a workflow

Start from any of the 100 in this catalog, or describe your own process in plain English and Twixy builds it as a workflow. Duplicate, tweak, and it becomes yours.

Point it at a client

Fill in a few inputs — the client, the niche, a profile link. The AI then works the browser like a trained assistant: researching, drafting, publishing, and reporting, step by step.

Review the output, not the process

Every run ends in a concrete deliverable — an audit spreadsheet, a content batch, a client-ready report — with validation checks along the way. You review results in minutes, not methods for hours.

Turn the messy first week into a repeatable battery of audits and briefs — identical quality for every client you sign.

Spotlight · what agencies run first

003

Instagram Account Deep Audit

30 posts analyzed: what works, what doesn't, and five quick wins.

Twixy opens the client's profile in a real browser and collects the last 30 posts — format, opening hook, hashtags, likes, comments, exact posting time. It computes per-post engagement against followers, then builds a two-sheet audit: raw data, plus per-format averages, the shared traits of the top 10% of posts, the weakest habits to stop, and five quick wins for week one.

You provide the profile link  ·  You get an audit spreadsheet ready for the kickoff call

010

Client Onboarding Summary Pack

Every audit merged into one kickoff deck for the client.

After the onboarding audits have run, their outputs are scattered across files. This workflow reads all of them — account audit, site snapshot, competitor set, voice guide — and merges them into a single kickoff pack: what we found, what it means, the direction we recommend. The "here's how thoroughly we analyzed you" document, produced automatically.

You provide the client name  ·  You get one kickoff-ready report pack

012

Quick-Wins Scan

Bio, links, pinned posts — fixes you can ship in week one.

Twixy reads the profile the way a senior strategist would — bio, link-in-bio (it clicks through and checks the landing page), pinned posts, highlights, recent grid — against a built-in review checklist. It only flags fixes that need no new content production. Each finding: current state → suggested change (with rewritten copy) → how to apply it and how long it takes.

You provide the profile link  ·  You get a week-one fix list, evidence attached
