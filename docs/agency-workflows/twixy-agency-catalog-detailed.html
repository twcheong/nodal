Built for the way small agencies actually break

Twixy was designed around the three problems every 2–10 person social media agency runs into as it grows.

Problem 01

The busywork tax

Every new client means the same research, the same spreadsheets, the same copy-paste relay between ten open tabs and an AI chat. Tasks take too long, errors slip in, and the process starts over with the next client.

Problem 02

Knowledge that walks out the door

Your best prompts and working methods live in someone's personal notes and memory. Output quality depends on who does the work — and when an experienced person leaves, their process leaves with them.

Problem 03

The founder bottleneck

Reviews, status-chasing, and client reporting all flow through one person. Approvals pile up, missed deadlines surface too late, and the founder's workload grows with every client added.

How Twixy works

A workflow is your agency's process captured as a runnable asset — visible steps, built-in quality checks, and a deliverable at the end.

Pick a workflow

Start from any of the 100 in this catalog, or describe your own process in plain English and Twixy builds it as a workflow. Duplicate, tweak, and it becomes yours.

Point it at a client

Fill in a few inputs — the client, the niche, a profile link. The AI then works the browser like a trained assistant: researching, drafting, publishing, and reporting, step by step.

Review the output, not the process

Every run ends in a concrete deliverable — an audit spreadsheet, a content batch, a client-ready report — with validation checks along the way. You review results in minutes, not methods for hours.

Instagram Account Deep AuditTop pick

30 posts analyzed: what works, what doesn't, and five quick wins.

How it works

Twixy opens the client's profile in a real browser and collects the last 30 posts in two passes — format, opening hook, hashtags, likes, comments, and the exact posting time of each. It computes per-post engagement against followers, then builds a two-sheet audit: the raw post data, plus an analysis sheet with average engagement by format, the shared traits of the top 10% of posts, the weakest habits to stop, and five quick wins for week one. A built-in validation step checks the data is complete before anything is scored.

Quick-Wins ScanTop pick

Bio, links, pinned posts — fixes you can ship in week one.

How it works

SEO Blog Post That OutranksTop pick

How it works

Three moves. First, Twixy opens the top 5–7 Google results for your target keyword and dissects them — title patterns, length, heading structure, keyword placement — and finds the content gaps none of them cover. Third, it writes the full 1,500+ word draft with image-slot directions and a meta description. Validation enforces length, heading count, and a keyword frequency in the healthy 5–15 range — no stuffing, no absence.

Brand Mention SweepTop pick

X, Threads, and Reddit mentions triaged with reply drafts.

How it works

Twixy searches X and Threads for the brand and product names — including replies and quote posts — then sweeps Reddit for threads and comments, prioritizing questions and complaints. Every mention gets two labels: sentiment (positive / neutral / negative) and urgency (respond today / this week / no action). The report leads with the urgent items, each with a drafted reply ready to adapt, followed by sentiment counts per platform and recurring themes worth a content response. One morning read and the account manager knows exactly what needs a reply.
