# Customer Preparation Checklist (Pilot)

One page, sent after the demo. Everything here is doable in under an hour of the customer's time.

## Before kickoff (required)

- [ ] **Pick your pilot workflow** — one repetitive task your team does for multiple clients (e.g. competitor research, content research, campaign monitoring, monthly report prep).
- [ ] **Name your champion** — the person who does this task today and will run it in Twixy.
- [ ] **Name one more user** — a teammate who will *reuse* the workflow in weeks 2–3 (this is how we test team value, not just personal value).
- [ ] **Pick 2–3 real clients** to run the pilot workflow on. Prefer clients whose data is not unusually sensitive.
- [ ] **Rough baseline guess** — how long does this task take today, and how often per month? (We'll measure precisely in week 1; a gut number is enough to start.)

## Machine & access (champion's computer)

- [ ] macOS or Windows computer that can install a desktop app (Twixy is a desktop workspace).
- [ ] **Google Chrome installed** — Twixy's automation runs through Chrome.
- [ ] Logins available for the websites the workflow touches (e.g. the social platforms you check during research). Twixy operates in its own Chrome profile; you sign in once during setup.
- [ ] Normal internet access to the sites involved (no VPN/firewall rules blocking them).

## Decisions to make (founder, 10 minutes)

- [ ] Agree the **success targets** with us before starting (we propose: time per task −50%, ≥5 reuse runs, founder review time −30% — see measurement framework).
- [ ] Decide who may view/run shared workflows in the team workspace.
- [ ] Skim the **Security & Privacy FAQ** and send us any questions before kickoff.

## Explicitly NOT required

- No IT department, no server setup, no integration project.
- No migration — keep your current PM/scheduling/reporting tools; the pilot runs alongside them.
- No change for clients — outputs are reviewed by your team before anything is client-facing.
- No long commitment — the pilot is 4 weeks and ends with your numbers on one page.
