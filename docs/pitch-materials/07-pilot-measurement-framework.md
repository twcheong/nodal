# Pilot Measurement Framework

How the pilot proves (or disproves) value in 4 weeks — using the metrics already defined in the customer-problem research. Numbers below marked *hypothesis* must be replaced with the customer's own baseline in Week 1.

## Principle

We measure **one repetitive workflow**, end to end, before and after — not "AI adoption" in the abstract. The founder picks the workflow; we agree on the numbers before the pilot starts.

## Metrics (tied to the three customer problems)

| Problem | Metric | How measured | Baseline (hypothesis) | Pilot target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Repetitive browser work | **Time per task** (e.g. competitor research per client) | Employee self-timed, 3 samples before / 3 during | 2–3 hrs/task | −50% or more |
| Repetitive browser work | **Weekly repetitive-work hours** per employee | 1-week time log (simple template provided) | 5–10 hrs/wk | −30% or more |
| Process depends on individuals | **Workflow reuse count** | Twixy dashboard: runs of the shared workflow by *other* team members | 0 (nothing reusable today) | ≥ 5 reuse runs by ≥ 2 people |
| Process depends on individuals | **Revision / rework count** on the output | Client-facing revisions per deliverable | Customer's current average | Equal or fewer |
| Founder review bottleneck | **Founder review & status-checking hours** per week | Founder self-report, before vs. Week 4 | > 5 hrs/wk | −30% or more |

**North-star check at the end:** "Could your current team take on one more client without hiring?" (yes/no + why)

## Measurement cadence

- **Week 1 (baseline):** time logs collected while the team works the old way; agree targets in writing.
- **Weeks 2–3 (run):** Twixy dashboard + execution logs provide run counts, completion/failure counts, and timestamps automatically; employees keep the light time log only for the pilot workflow.
- **Week 4 (readout):** one-page before/after scorecard; founder decision meeting.

## Data sources

| Source | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Twixy workflow status dashboard | Runs, completions, failures, pending |
| Twixy execution logs | Actions, results, errors, timestamps per run |
| 1-page time-log template (we provide) | Human hours before vs. during |
| Founder interview (15 min, Week 1 & 4) | Review hours, confidence, decision criteria |

## Success definition (agree before starting)

The pilot is a success if **at least two of three** hold:
1. Time per pilot task down ≥ 50%
2. The workflow was reused by someone other than its builder ≥ 5 times
3. Founder review/status time down ≥ 30%

## Honest failure conditions

We also agree what would make us say it didn't work: workflow fails more than ~1 in 5 runs after configuration, outputs need more revision than the manual version, or the team stops using it by Week 3. If that happens, we say so — and the execution logs will show why.
