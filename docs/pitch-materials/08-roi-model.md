# ROI Model — Twixy for a 2–10 Person Social Media Agency

A simple, founder-facing model. All inputs are **editable assumptions** — replace with the customer's numbers during the pilot (Week 1 baseline). Never present outputs as guarantees; present the *model* and let the founder plug in their own numbers.

## Example agency (base case)

| Assumption | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Team size | 5 people (founder + 4) | Target segment midpoint |
| Fully-loaded cost per marketer | CAD $40/hr (≈ $70k/yr) | Toronto junior–mid agency rate |
| Repetitive browser/research/reporting work | 6 hrs/person/week | Research hypothesis: 5–10 hrs |
| Share of that work Twixy can automate (configured workflows) | 50% (conservative) | To validate in pilot |
| Founder review & status-checking time | 6 hrs/week | Research hypothesis: > 5 hrs |
| Founder review time saved | 30% | To validate in pilot |
| Clients served | 8 | Typical for segment |

## Value calculation (base case)

**Team time saved**
4 staff × 6 hrs/wk × 50% = **12 hrs/week** → 12 × $40 × 4.33 = **≈ $2,080/month**

**Founder time saved**
6 hrs/wk × 30% = **1.8 hrs/week** of the founder's time back → redirected to sales/clients (value above staff rate; count conservatively at $60/hr ≈ **$470/month**)

**Total modeled value: ≈ $2,500/month** (≈ 14 staff-hours + ~2 founder-hours weekly)

## Cost side

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Twixy team subscription (5 seats) | *[insert current pricing]* /month |
| Setup effort | ~2–4 hrs in pilot week 1 (with our help, see onboarding doc) |
| Learning curve | Marketers run shared workflows without building from scratch |

**Modeled payback:** if subscription < $2,500/month, ROI is positive in month 1 at base-case assumptions; at half the assumed savings it still covers a typical team subscription several times over.

## The comparison the founder actually makes: Twixy vs. the next hire

| | Junior hire | Twixy |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cost | ≈ $4,500–5,500 (salary + overhead) | Team subscription |
| Time to productive | 1–3 months onboarding | Pilot: first workflow in week 1 |
| Capacity added | ~1 person, one skill set | Frees hours across the whole existing team |
| Knowledge risk | Leaves with the person | Workflows stay with the agency |

**Framing:** "Twixy doesn't replace hiring — it delays your *next* hire until it's for growth, not for keeping up."

| Conservative | 30% | ≈ $1,500 |

## Rules for using this model

- Label every number as an assumption until the pilot baseline replaces it.
- Do **not** promise "X% workload reduction" — the pilot measurement framework (doc 07) exists precisely to measure the customer's own number.
- Currency: CAD for Toronto prospects; adjust rates by market.
