Step 1 of 6

Welcome aboard.

This wizard installs OpenClaw — your own personal AI assistant that runs locally on your machine. You'll be chatting with your own agent in about five minutes.

  • Installs the OpenClaw CLI globally via npm.
  • Configures your AI model provider of choice.
  • Starts the local Gateway daemon so it keeps running in the background.

Click Next to continue. You can cancel at any time.
